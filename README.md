# Paw & Whiskers – Pet Care

A responsive, modern web application for **Paw & Whiskers – Pet Care**, designed with a soft pastel color palette (sage green, cream, beige, and soft peach), rounded cards, smooth scroll animations, and an interactive appointment booking form.

**Live Demo:** [https://sanjanachowdary-0410.github.io/Paw-Whiskers-Pet-Care/](https://sanjanachowdary-0410.github.io/Paw-Whiskers-Pet-Care/)

---

## Features & Sections

1. **Navigation Bar**
   - Fixed header with brand logo, smooth anchor scrolling, and a responsive mobile hamburger menu.
   - Quick navigation to **Home**, **Services**, **Pet Care Tips**, **Our Team**, **Contact**, and **Book Now**.

2. **Home / Hero Section**
   - Welcome heading (*"Happy, Healthy Pets Start at Paw & Whiskers"*), tagline (*"Where Every Tail Wags & Every Purr Matters"*), clinic introduction, and key stats (`4,500+` happy pets cared for, `12+ yrs` veterinary excellence, `4.9 ★` rating).
   - **Book an Appointment** and **Explore Services** action buttons alongside dog and cat photo cards and a *Fear-Free Certified Clinic* badge.

3. **Available Services**
   - Service cards with icons, descriptions, and direct booking links that pre-select the chosen service in the appointment form:
     - **Veterinary Check-Ups**
     - **Vaccination**
     - **Pet Grooming & Bathing**
     - **Pet Training**
     - **Nutrition Guidance**
     - **Pet Daycare**

4. **Pet Care Tips**
   - Wellness cards covering **Healthy Feeding Habits**, **Importance of Fresh Water**, **Grooming & Hygiene**, **Exercise & Playtime**, and **Regular Health Check-Ups**.

5. **Veterinarian & Trainer Information**
   - Profiles featuring photographs, credentials, experience, and specializations:
     - **Dr. Clara Bennett** (Chief Veterinarian — *DVM, MS • 12 Years Experience*)
     - **Dr. Julian Vance** (Senior Veterinarian — *DVM, DACVN • 9 Years Experience*)
     - **Maya Lin** (Lead Pet Trainer — *CPDT-KA • 8 Years Experience*)

6. **Interactive Appointment Booking**
   - Collects **Owner's Name**, **Contact Number**, **Pet's Name**, **Pet Type & Age**, **Required Service**, **Preferred Date**, **Preferred Time**, and an optional **Additional Message**.
   - Includes client-side validation (required fields, minimum phone digit length, non-past appointment dates) and displays a formatted confirmation message upon submission.

7. **Contact Information & Map**
   - Displays clinic address (`742 Meadowbrook Lane, Suite 102, Willow Creek, OR 97205`), phone numbers, email addresses, working hours (`Mon – Fri: 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM`, `Sat – Sun: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM`), social media links, and an embedded Google Map.

8. **Footer**
   - Brand summary, quick navigation links, contact details, and copyright notice (`© 2026 Paw & Whiskers – Pet Care`).

---

## Built With

- **HTML5** — Semantic section structure and form elements
- **CSS3** — CSS custom properties (variables), CSS Grid & Flexbox layouts, responsive media queries, hover transitions, and scroll-reveal animations
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)** — Mobile navigation drawer toggle, service pre-selection, date minimums, `IntersectionObserver` scroll animations, and form validation/confirmation
- **Google Fonts & Font Awesome** — `Fredoka` and `Nunito` typography plus icon set
