Daily Wellness Guide

Helpful Pet Care Tips

Simple, vet-approved habits that keep your furry companions thriving, energized, and full of joy every single day.

Healthy Feeding Habits

Feed measured, age-appropriate portions on a consistent schedule rather than free-feeding. Limit treats to under 10% of daily calories to maintain a healthy weight.

Importance of Fresh Water

Refresh water bowls at least twice daily and wash bowls with mild soap. Proper hydration supports kidney function, digestion, and shiny coats—especially for cats.

Grooming & Hygiene

Brush your pet’s coat weekly to prevent matting and distribute natural skin oils. Pair regular brushing with weekly tooth brushing and monthly nail trims.

Exercise & Playtime

Provide daily physical activity and puzzle toys for mental stimulation. Interactive play reduces boredom, prevents destructive habits, and strengthens your bond.

Regular Health Check-Ups

Schedule annual wellness visits (or bi-annual for senior pets) even when your pet looks healthy. Preventive screenings detect subtle changes before symptoms appear.
